Dependable Lift Servicing & Maintenance Throughout the North West

New Lift Installations

Our lift company specialises in the installation of all lifts. These include:

  • Passenger lifts
  • Goods lifts
  • Platform lifts and access lifts
  • Car lifts
  • Food lifts
  • Dumbwaiter lifts
More Information →

Planned Maintenance & Servicing

Failure to keep up with regular maintenance not only involves an increased cost, it also compromises the reliability of the lift. To prevent this, our elevator company offers the following services:

  • Regular maintenance
  • Compliance with Health & Safety requirements
  • Advice and planning of future repairs
  • UK stock for replacement parts
More Information →

Modernise Your Lifts

A modernisation can give just as good of a result as a new installation, for a more cost-effective price. If your lift is suitable for modernisation, the benefits include:

  • Reduced downtime
  • Improved appearance & image
  • Greater ride quality
  • Minimal disruption in the building
  • Reduced running costs
More Information →

Breakdowns & Repairs

Breakdowns can be a real problem for everyone impacted. When choosing Altius Lifts for your lift needs, our lift company will do the following:

  • Conduct a free site survey
  • Issue a comprehensive report on our findings
  • Highlight any safety issues or components at risk
  • Provide an action plan for the best course of action
  • Complete the repairs to the highest standard
